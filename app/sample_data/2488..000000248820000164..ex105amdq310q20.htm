Capitalized terms used in this Fourth Amendment without definition shall have the meanings set forth in the Original WSA and in Wafer Supply Agreement Amendment No. 1 dated as of April 2, 2011, Wafer Supply Agreement Amendment No. 2 dated as of March 4, 2012 (the “Second Amendment”) or Wafer Supply Agreement Amendment No. 3 dated as of December 6, 2012 (the “Third Amendment”), as applicable.
