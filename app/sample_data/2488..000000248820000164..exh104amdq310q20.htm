1.Payments and Maturity. Maker shall pay two hundred million dollars ($200,000,000) on or before December 31, 2013 (such repayments together with any Default Interest, the “Obligations”).
2.Interest. No interest shall be payable on this Note; provided that, except as set forth in Section 4 below, if (a) any Obligation is not paid in full in cash in immediately available funds into the bank account set forth on Exhibit A on or before its due date, or (b) any other Event of Default shall occur and remain uncured, then without any further action on the part of the Payee interest shall immediately become due and payable on the aggregate unpaid principal amount that has not been paid by Maker when due pursuant to this Note at such time, at the rate of twenty percent (20%) per annum (the “Default Interest”), commencing retroactively from the date of this Note as set forth above ([ ], 2012) and accruing until the date on which both such unpaid and overdue principal amount and any unpaid Default Interest have been paid in full and no other Event of Default shall remain uncured.
3.Prepayment. Maker may prepay this Note at any time, in whole or in part, without notice, penalty, or premium.
4.Payments.
